## 4b. Label-side baseline comparison — AMEO (within-family head-to-head)
Source: `outputs/gap/metrics/labelside_baseline.json`, `outputs/gap/tables/labelside_headtohead.csv` (gap09; same merit covariates, same gap_core OOF protocol, SEED=42, `n_jobs=1`; **bit-reproducible**, identical across two runs, md5 `8ccd86c1…`). All methods **audited against the realized label** for apples-to-apples. The `no_correction` and `dglc` rows reproduce DGLC regimes A and C above exactly (0.47022 / 0.74128 and 0.54859 / 0.74240).

| Method | Family | Women sel. | Men sel. | Lift vs base | Dir. | AUROC (realized) | EO diff vs realized | Oaxaca residual after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no correction | — | 0.47022 | 0.40875 | 0.00000 | flat | 0.74128 | 0.09359 | +0.06988 |
| post-hoc eq-odds | model-side | 0.43469 | 0.41730 | −0.03553 | down | n/a | 0.05419 | +0.06988 |

| Massaging (Kamiran–Calders) | label-side | 0.45455 | 0.41434 | −0.01567 | down | 0.74644 | 0.06768 | +0.06988 |
| **DGLC** | label-side (decomp.) | **0.54859** | 0.47846 | **+0.07837** | up | 0.74240 | 0.09983 | **≈0 (2.46e−14)** |

DGLC merit-implied target rate (`dglc_merit_implied_rate`): women **0.549** vs men **0.478**. Massaging relabelled **M=26** per side (`massaging_meta`).

**Honest reading (DGLC does *not* uniformly dominate):**
- **Levelling direction is not a clean family split.** Only DGLC produces a large upward correction (**+0.078**); the parity-targeting label-side/data-centric baselines barely move (Reweighing **+0.006**) or go **down** (Massaging **−0.016**), as do the model-side methods. So the manuscript's "post-hoc levels DOWN vs label-side lifts UP" contrast is too simple — a label-side parity baseline (Massaging) also goes down.
- **DGLC is worst on realized-label equalized-odds (0.100), by design.** It deliberately deviates from the realized label toward merit, so judged against the realized label it does not reduce the EO gap; post-hoc (0.054), EG (0.062), and Massaging (0.068) do. DGLC targets the *merit-implied* rate, not parity with the (biased) realized label.
- **AUROC is preserved comparably** by Reweighing (0.742), Massaging (0.746), and DGLC (0.742); EG drops it to 0.689.
- **Self-validation is the clean DGLC differentiator:** only DGLC zeroes the Oaxaca unexplained residual (+0.070 → ≈0); Reweighing and Massaging leave it at **+0.070** because they never modify the continuous wage structure (`self_validation` block in the JSON).
